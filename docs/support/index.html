Common questions

How do submissions get approved?
Every deal, bar, or event you submit goes into a moderation queue and is reviewed by a real person before it goes live. Approved submissions earn you points; if something's rejected, you'll see the reviewer's note explaining why in your submission history.

I forgot my password.
From the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and follow the prompts — you'll get a one-time code by email to reset it.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → scroll down → Delete Account. This permanently removes your personal information and can't be undone.

I found a bug or something looks wrong.
Email us at the address above with what happened and, if you can, a screenshot — that helps us track it down fast.
